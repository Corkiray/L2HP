## Domain
I have to plan logistics to transport packages within cities via trucks and between cities via airplanes. Locations within a city are directly connected (trucks can move between any two such locations), and so are the cities. In each city there is exactly one truck and each city has one location that serves as an airport.

Let's analyze the different ways to accomplish this delivery task:

1. Local Delivery:
   - When source and destination are in the same city
   - Only requires a truck
   
2. Inter-city Delivery:
   - When source and destination are in different cities
   - Requires truck and airplane coordination
   
3. Special Cases:
   - Direct airport-to-airport delivery
   - Multi-hop delivery through intermediate cities
